Joe Raio is a Technical Program & Product Director at Microsoft, where he works with top Financial Services organizations to design and build innovative cloud solutions on Microsoft Azure.

A Miami native transplanted to Redmond, Washington, Joe is an active member of the developer community — speaking at local user groups, code camps, and international events such as [Code Europe](https://codeeurope.pl/) and [Functions World](https://www.functions.world/). During his time at Microsoft he has received honors including Share Fighter Champion and Americas Gold Club.

Outside of work, Joe is a lifelong guitarist and singer, a recovering gear collector, and a perpetual home-lab tinkerer. He lives with his wife and two daughters.

## What I'm up to

- **Day job** — Technical Program & Product Director at Microsoft, helping top Financial Services organizations adopt Azure.
- **Speaking** — user groups, code camps, and conferences including Code Europe and Functions World.
- **Projects** — I build the occasional weekend project, like a [mobile-first client for the HDHomeRun TV tuner]({% post_url 2026-08-26-hdhomerun-web %}) and a [download automation tool for my home server]({% post_url 2025-07-20-ytpi %}).
- **Music** — singer and guitarist of the South Florida metal band [Scars of Life](/music/).
